Pair both structural conditions with the same representative transcript and the same transcript position in the frozen HEK293T GLORI site; ±10 nt on both sides are required to achieve 70% effective coverage in both conditions. Finally, 3,705 loci and 1,075 genes were retained.

The associated effect per SD was 0.010862 (95% CI 0.001990–0.019735) in vivo; 0.002386 (95% CI -0.006233–0.011006) in vitro. The paired gene bootstrap difference in vivo minus in vitro was 0.008476 (95% CI 0.001517–0.015189).

The present results belong to an observational paired analysis initiated post hoc. Conditional differences can be used to distinguish consistency of structural environment but cannot alone demonstrate a causal role for intracellular environment or RNA structure on m6A.
